About

The operating system hospitality actually deserves.

EatOps is built for the people who run great cafés and restaurants — and are tired of holding it all together with group chats and spreadsheets.

Walk into most growing café groups and you'll find the same thing: orders, inventory alerts, staff updates and the day's chaos all buried in the same WhatsApp group as lunch plans. The tools don't talk to each other. The POS doesn't know the inventory. Nobody can see what's happening at outlet three until it's already gone wrong.

We built EatOps to fix exactly that — a single, connected system for menu, ordering, loyalty, inventory, team chat and assets. Each piece is a module you can switch on as you need it, so a single café and a multi-brand group both get a system that fits.

And here's the part we care about most: nothing is off-the-shelf. Your café isn't a template, so your software shouldn't be one either. We shape every screen, menu and flow to your identity — your name, your colours, your way of working — so EatOps feels like a part of your brand, not someone else's product.

EatOps is a product of Crestify, a Kaart Studio division. We're currently in build with a handful of select hospitality groups across India.
