The product

Six modules.
One connected system.

Pick the modules your operation needs — menu, loyalty, inventory, chat, assets, access — and run them all from one place, shaped to your brand.

01 / DIGITAL MENU & ORDERING

A menu guests actually enjoy using

Scan a QR code, browse with photos, and order in seconds. Your team sees every order land in a live queue.

  • Edit your menu live, no developer needed
  • Sort items into categories with drag-to-reorder
  • Order at the table by QR — dine-in or takeaway
  • Live order queue with real-time status updates
  • "86" a sold-out item instantly
MENU · QR ORDERING
02 / LOYALTY & REWARDS

Keep guests coming back

Points, tiers and birthday perks — all handled automatically, and tamper-proof because points are awarded server-side.

  • Points on every order
  • Members move up tiers automatically
  • A catalogue of rewards to redeem
  • Birthday & happy-hour offers
  • Configurable rules per brand
LOYALTY · TIERS
03 / INVENTORY

Know what you have, right now

Real-time stock at every outlet, with alerts before you run low and an audit trail that can never be edited.

  • Stock levels for each outlet
  • Par-level alerts before you run out
  • Every stock in & out, logged immutably
  • Track wastage by reason
  • Move stock between outlets
INVENTORY · PAR LEVELS
04 / SECURE TEAM CHAT

One channel for everything

Organised by outlet and role, private and fully audited. Leak-resistant by design — not buried in a WhatsApp group.

  • Separate channels for each team
  • Screenshots blocked at the OS level on Android
  • Downloads require approval
  • Every message kept on record
CHAT · SECURE CHANNEL
05 / ASSET & OUTLET MANAGEMENT

Every machine, tracked

From the espresso machine to the patio chairs — scan a QR to see history or report damage, with a full audit trail.

  • A QR code on every asset
  • Scan to report damage with a photo
  • An asset registry for each outlet
  • Full history of every change
  • Built for multiple brands & outlets
ASSETS · QR
06 / ROLES & ACCESS

Exactly the right access

Owners see the whole group; staff see only their outlet. Enforced at the database, not just hidden in the interface.

  • Group → Brand → Outlet → User hierarchy
  • Four role levels, from staff to super admin
  • Switch between outlets instantly
  • Database-level security on every record
ACCESS · ROLES
