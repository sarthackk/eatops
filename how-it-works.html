The guest's side

Five steps, and none of them are your job.

From the moment someone sits down to the moment the order reaches you, your team doesn't have to do a thing.

01 — SITS DOWN

One code on the table.

No paper menu to wait for, no app to download. They point a camera at the square on the table — the one thing everybody already knows how to do.

02 — IT OPENS

The menu is just there.

It opens straight in the browser in about a second. Nothing to install, no sign-up, no account, no "continue with Google".

03 — BROWSES

They see what they're ordering.

A photo, a description and the price on every item. One tap filters to vegan, gluten free or spicy — so nobody has to ask, and nobody has to remember the answer.

04 — ORDERS

Ordering without getting up.

Tap to add, tap to send. Anything you've run out of shows as sold out before they pick it, so the disappointment never happens.

05 — CONFIRMED

Confirmed on their screen.

They see exactly what they ordered and what it costs, and can add to it from the same menu whenever they like — without flagging anyone down.

Your side

Meanwhile, at the counter.

The same order that took a guest four taps arrives with you already sorted, already priced, already counted against your stock.

It arrives with the table on it

No shouting across the pass, no guessing which order belongs to which table. The ticket lands the second it's placed.

Stock counts itself down

Every order subtracts what it used. When something runs low you hear about it days early, not when a guest asks for it.

The menu stays honest

Mark something sold out once and it greys out on every phone in the room. No reprinting, no crossing things off.

And the rest of it

The menu is one of six.

Orders, loyalty, inventory, team chat and equipment run on the same system and talk to each other — which is the entire point. The QR menu is just the part your guests can see.
